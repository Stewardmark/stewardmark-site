Your staff are already using AI, whether or not you have a policy.  Governance does not need to slow that down.  Done well, it tells people which tools they can use, how to use them safely, and whom to ask, so they can move faster with less risk.

## What an AI readiness review covers

- Which AI tools are in use today, approved or not.
- Where sensitive data could leave the organization, and how to prevent it.
- Vendor terms for data retention, model training, and security.
- The questions clients, funders, insurers, and regulators are likely to ask, with answers ready.

## What you receive

- A written AI use policy sized to your organization.
- A short list of approved tools and the settings that make them safe to use.
- A lightweight process for reviewing new tools and new uses.
- Training for staff on the policy and on using AI well.
