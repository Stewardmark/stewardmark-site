Many organizations already have a capable IT director or technology manager who has never had a CIO to learn from.  Mentoring helps that person grow into the leader you need, often at a fraction of the cost of hiring one.

## What we work on

- Building and presenting a technology budget.
- Writing a roadmap that leadership will approve.
- Managing vendors and managed service providers.
- Hiring, developing, and leading a team.
- Communicating with executives and the board.
- Leading AI adoption with confidence.

## How it works

We meet one-on-one on a regular schedule.  Each session focuses on the real problems in front of your technology leader that week.

## Why me

I began my career building networks and donor systems at an environmental nonprofit and worked my way to CIO.  Over my years leading a 26-person team, I hired nearly every member and developed several people from the help desk into managers.  I know what that climb takes, and I enjoy helping others make it.
