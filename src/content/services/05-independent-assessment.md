Large technology decisions often rest on advice from the people selling the product.  An independent assessment gives you a second opinion from someone with no stake in the outcome.  I take no vendor commissions, markups, or resale margins.

## What I assess

- **Platforms.**  Accounting, document management, CRM, and collaboration systems, either before you buy or when the current system is not working.
- **Contracts.**  Renewals, managed service agreements, and software licensing, with attention to cost, terms, and exit rights.
- **Roadmaps.**  An existing technology plan, checked for gaps, risks, sequencing, and cost.
- **Security posture.**  A practical review of controls, backups, and recovery readiness.

## What you receive

You receive a written report in plain language with findings, the options available to you, and a clear recommendation.  I walk your leadership team through it and answer questions.

## When it helps most

- Before signing a large or multi-year contract.
- When a managed service provider is not meeting expectations.
- When a board or leadership team wants an outside view before approving a budget.
- After a leadership change, when you need to know where things really stand.
